## Doubts
- FR names: fr.wowhead.com redirects in a loop through fetch; no French NPC or zone page seen. Only Azuregos (unchanged) and "gardien Remulos" have French evidence.
- Wowhead zone pages return no NPC lists via fetch; rare/elite classification of Geltharis, Legashi Satyr, Kaya Flathoof not confirmed.
- Coverage gaps (no verified source found): Teldrassil commons (Gnarlpine, spiders, nightsabers) and Darnassus NPCs beyond Tyrande; Darkshore rares, epics, legendary; Ashenvale rares and legendary; Stonetalon commons, rares, epic, legendary; Azshara rares and elites; Felwood creatures, rares, elite, legendary; Winterspring rares and legendary; Moonglade commons.
- Blackfathom Deeps as a "place" row has no dedicated URL seen (only the Aku'mai and Je'neu quest pages). Lord Kazzak is in Blasted Lands, deliberately excluded.
- Levels quoted come from Classic pages; Season of Discovery and Forever versions may differ.
- uiMapIDs resolved from the Classic table on https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/UiMapID; the memory guesses in the old Doubts line matched except that none was Classic (those were retail IDs 57, 62, 63...).
- New rows come from the warcraft.wiki.gg Classic zone pages (Moonglade page is the all-version page; its "(Classic)" title redirects). Zone pages list no rare mobs; rare rows come from an old wiki user page (https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/User:FuzzicalLogic/Maps/Rare_Spawn_Locations), names only, levels not trusted.
- No rare rows could be sourced for Teldrassil (only Blackmoss already carded), Stonetalon, Winterspring or Moonglade: the user rare page does not cover them.
- No Epic for Darkshore, Stonetalon (only a micro-dungeon place), Winterspring or Felwood beyond elite areas/NPCs. Thundris Windweaver (Darkshore Legendary) is only the page-listed ruler, not clearly iconic.
- Dragons of Nightmare and Highlord-type world bosses: flagged; whether they are live at Forever launch was not checked. Darnassus is carded as a place under Teldrassil per the folding rule.
