## Forever additions
Nothing found inside Swamp of Sorrows, Blasted Lands or Deadwind Pass. Eastern Kingdoms additions elsewhere, per search results (not opened in full, summary from search):
- Riverglades: new east-coast zone, 150+ quests, mid-30s to mid-40s.
- Zephras Isle: floating Skyborne start zone (counted inconsistently as a zone).
- New dungeons: Hall of Thanes (13-18), Ruins of Lordaeron (15-20), Excavation Site (24-29), City of Dalaran (28-33), The Drowned City (35-40), Krol'dok Stronghold.
- Stormwind Harbor to Auberdine route opened.
- Launch date 4 Nov 2026.

## Doubts
- FR names: only Marais des Chagrins verified (fr page). Others "?"; the FR zone names for Blasted Lands and Deadwind Pass were not verified.
- Spec quotas (Common creature families, Epic/Legendary) are not met: Wowhead pages exposed no creature lists. Common rows (crocolisks, turtles, murlocs, basilisks, ogres, etc.) need a manual pass.
- Rare rows: Ravage, Deatheye, Grunter had retail/MoP URLs or mixed levels; Classic availability unconfirmed for Ravage and Deatheye.
- Itharius: sources conflict on location (one cached page says unknown); Swamp of Sorrows per search summary.
- Temple of Atal'Hakkar URL is the retail zone=1477 page; Classic zone page not seen.
- Archmage Allistarj zone from page fetch; no coordinates.
- Mor'Ladim is Duskwood, excluded. Kargath, Dark Portal, Stonard, Fallen Hero of the Horde, Medivh, Kel'Thuzad not found in Classic results.
- Forever info is from search summaries, not full articles; zone count (three vs four) disputed.
- uiMapIDs resolved from the Classic table on https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/UiMapID; the older "zone id" values in headings were Wowhead zone ids and are replaced.
- Swamp of Sorrows, Blasted Lands and Deadwind Pass rows added from warcraft.wiki.gg Classic zone pages. The Sunken Temple wiki page opened was the retail roster, so no new temple bosses were added.
- Deadwind Pass: the wiki page lists no rare mobs, no dungeons and no NPCs except Medivh (dead in lore); Medivh is a lore row, not a spawn. No verified Rare/Epic row.
- Lord Kazzak is carded as a flagged world boss only because the Blasted Lands page names him ruler; his world-boss status in Forever was not checked.
- Swamp of Sorrows rares come from an old wiki user page (https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/User:FuzzicalLogic/Maps/Rare_Spawn_Locations); names only, levels there look unreliable. Blasted Lands rares already in the file were left as they were.
