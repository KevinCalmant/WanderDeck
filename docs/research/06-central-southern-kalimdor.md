## Doubts
- Wowhead pages are JS-rendered: WebFetch returned no spawn or NPC lists, and a direct curl was refused by the sandbox (bot-check rule), so I did not retry. Content comes from search snippets plus my memory.
- Coverage is partial and well below the ~12-15 target for most zones. Not padded with guesses. Still to research: Zul'Farrak, Ahn'Qiraj raid rows (Ruins/Temple), Cenarion Hold, Marshal's Refuge, Gordunni Outpost, Mosh'Ogg, all rare spawns, dungeon bosses (WC, RFK, Maraudon, DM, ZF), creature families for Thousand Needles, Desolace, Dustwallow, Feralas, Tanaris, Un'Goro, Silithus.
- Rows citing only a zone/quest/guide page rely on memory for the entity itself (own page not opened). Bael'dun Digsite, Rau Cliffrunner, Taragaman and creature families are in this group.
- Mulgore zone=215 page never appeared in results, so it is not cited. Dropped Mazzranache and Regthar Deathgate (unverified) from the draft.
- All FR names marked `~` are unverified. Gazz'uz, Vol'jin, Thrall, Cavernes des Lamentations, Maraudon, Gadgetzan, Orgrimmar, Silithus, Tanaris are identical in FR by my memory, still unconfirmed on fr.wowhead. Only Gouffre de Ragefeu was seen in a French Wowhead title.
- Razorfen Downs zone: sources conflict. A Classic Wowhead quest page snippet places it in the Barrens; Icy Veins puts it at the Barrens' southern tip beside the Great Lift; warcraft.wiki.gg Classic lists Thousand Needles; a Chinese Wowhead guide says northern Barrens. Wowhead's own zone parent was unreadable. Placed in The Barrens (unconfirmed).
- Entrances (search snippets): Razorfen Kraul southern Barrens; Wailing Caverns Lushwater Oasis (Barrens); Maraudon Valley of Spears (Desolace); Onyxia's Lair southern Dustwallow; Ragefire Chasm in Orgrimmar (Cleft of Shadow), thus Durotar set per project rule.
- uiMapIDs come from memory, not Wowhead. Forever/Classic+ data may differ from Classic Era (level ranges differ across Wowhead versions).
- Thousand Needles zone page cited is the Forever one; Classic page not seen. Un'Goro/Silithus/Barrens/Durotar Classic zone pages seen only as search results.
