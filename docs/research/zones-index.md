## Gaps

- uiMapIDs: none missing. All 33 values are copied from the notes' Classic-table IDs, so no "?" is needed.
- Legendary "none" (14 zones): Loch Modan, Wetlands, Searing Gorge, Silverpine Forest, Hillsbrad Foothills, Alterac Mountains, Arathi Highlands, The Hinterlands, Western Plaguelands, Stonetalon Mountains, Felwood, Winterspring, The Barrens, Thousand Needles. Notes say these lack a verified rarity-5 figure, except Loch Modan and Wetlands, which have none sourced.
- Weak or flagged legendary picks: Medivh (lore only), Thundris Windweaver (ruler listed, not confirmed iconic), Lord Kazzak, Dragons of Nightmare and Azuregos (world bosses, live status in Forever not checked), King Bangalash (TBC page only), Chief Ukorz Sandscalp and Lar'korwi (judgement calls).
- Dungeon and raid rows that need checking: Razorfen Downs zone placement (sources conflict, placed in The Barrens); Karazhan (listed as a place, not confirmed as an instance); The Talon Den, Alcaz Island and Mo'grosh Stronghold (micro-dungeons, not confirmed as instances); Sunken Temple used the retail zone page; Naxxramas, Onyxia's Lair and the Ahn'Qiraj raids are flagged raids with no roster in the notes.
- Dungeon bosses without a verified roster: Scholomance and Shadowfang Keep (only the final boss is listed), and Stratholme beyond Baron Rivendare.
- Rares and commons are thin or unverified in several zones: Hillsbrad, Arathi, The Hinterlands and both Plaguelands have no verified rare list; Darkshore, Stonetalon, Winterspring and Moonglade have no rares sourced; Thousand Needles, Desolace, Dustwallow, Feralas, Tanaris, Un'Goro and Silithus creature families are not fully sourced. Rare rows for many zones come from an old user page (names only, levels not trusted).
- Zones with the fewest verified entries: Duskwood (family-level creature cards only), Stranglethorn Vale (family-level commons), Deadwind Pass (no verified rare or epic).
- French names are not part of this index. Most FR names in the notes are "?" or unverified.
