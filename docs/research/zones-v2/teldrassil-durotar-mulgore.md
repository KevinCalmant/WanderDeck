## Doubts
- Counts: Teldrassil 41 new rows (55 with shipped), Durotar 40 (54), Mulgore 37 (52). Trim the lowest-interest places if ~50 is strict.
- Wiki pages never print IDs in text; all IDs come from Wowhead/WoWDB link targets on the page. Keep them unverified in game until probed.
- Zone pages list no rares/elites by name (except The Rake in Mulgore, already shipped); every rare/elite row comes from the individual NPC page. Lady Sathrah, Githyiss and Ferocitas pages say "named mob", not rare: the rare tag is mine.
- Oakenscowl elite status: Classic only (removed 2.3.0). Ursal elite status not stated on the page.
- Jergosh the Invoker: the wiki page fetched returned a different NPC (id 109608, Caer Darrow), so no npcID is given. Item 14151 (Chanting Blade) is from its own page.
- Tears of the Moon 2518, Winterhoof Cleansing 24440 and Gnarlpine Corruption 476 come from link targets on pages that mix retail and Classic; the Classic variant (Tears of the Moon level 12) may have another ID. The Poison Water page gave 20440 (Cataclysm-era Horde quest), not used.
- Dwarven Digging 746 is explicitly labelled Classic on its page. Journey into Thunder Bluff 775 is carried over from 06 (Wowhead URL), not rechecked here.
- Kzan Thornslash is a minor NPC (one Forever quest); swap out if a better one is wanted.
- Harpy, Satyr, Thunder Lizard, Kodo, Palemane Gnoll are families from zone pages with no mob page or ID. Worgen, boar variants and Makrura were left out as unverified for Classic.
- Stone Guard Kurjack (44217) and Zelemar the Wrathful were left out: Cataclysm-era or summoned, uncertain in Forever.
- Eitrigg, Darnassus innkeeper and flight master: no ID shown. Eitrigg skipped. Caylais Moonfeather (3841) is Darkshore, not used.
- Orgrimmar, Eitrigg, Hamuul and Baine pages were truncated at 100k chars; later parts unread.
