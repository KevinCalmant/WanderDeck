## Doubts
- IDs come from Wowhead/WoWDB external links printed on the wiki pages; Wowhead itself was not opened. Rows tagged (01) reuse IDs from the earlier doc's Wowhead URLs, not re-checked. Boss/NPC IDs for Hamhock, Dextren Ward, Bazil Thredd come from their wiki pages.
- Bellygrub (npc 345) is a Lakeshire/Redridge rare, excluded.
- Kobold Candle (58903) is a Cataclysm-era item, excluded. Hogger's Trousers (1934): page says it drops from Hogger in the Stockade, unclear, excluded.
- Defias Overseer 48421 is a Cataclysm version; the Classic ID was not found, so no row.
- The Coastal Menace gives quest ID 26349 (Cataclysm-era, Old Murk-Eye) and Grayson's Torch (1172) is its reward: both excluded. The shipped Old Murk-Eye card may not exist in Forever; verify in game.
- Rockslicer (872) page says it drops from Admiral Ripsnarl (Cataclysm); Emberstone Staff source not confirmed; both excluded.
- Garrick Padfoot (103) is level 5 and not labeled rare on his page, so kind is npc. Thuros Lightfingers is listed as rare in Elwynn per its page.
- Murloc Tidehunter 127: page names only Westfall; mob level-scaling note looks odd. Defias Henchman 594: page says it was moved from the Deadmines to Sentinel Hill.
- Westfall Stew has an item ID (733); the quest ID is not shown. Thor lists a second ID (110587), not Classic; used 523. Innkeeper Farley lists 295 (Elwynn) and 250511 (modern); used 295.
- The Stockade (Classic) wiki page returned 404 and the Westfall quests page 404; Stockade bosses were taken from boss pages and Westfall quests from individual pages.
- Rarity counts overshoot the guide slightly: new Elwynn 23 C / 10 U / 5 R / 3 E; new Westfall 25 C / 8 U / 5 R / 3 E; no legendary added. Combined with shipped cards the epic count is 4 and 5 (Elwynn 4, Westfall 5). Trim commons or demote an epic if exact counts matter.
