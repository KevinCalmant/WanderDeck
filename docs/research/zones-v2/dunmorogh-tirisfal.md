## Doubts
- Counts exceed target: about 45 new rows per zone, so the caller can trim; the shipped sets (17 and 16) bring totals past 50.
- Quest IDs 24490, 26854 (Dun Morogh) and 24973, 25003 (Tirisfal) look like Cataclysm-era IDs; the wiki does not say whether Classic/Forever use them. Verify before use.
- Gnomish Rebreather ID 151081 looks wrong for Classic (page may mix versions). Discombobulator Ray 4388 is the crafted item, not the schematic. Wendigo Fur Cloak 3008 and Bloodstained Knife 3225 come from wiki links, unchecked in-game.
- Fellicent's Bindings 283471 and Whitemane's Chapeau 7720 (Forever 217300) are version-specific; Forever IDs may differ for other items too.
- NPC pages often list several IDs without labels (Innkeeper Renee: 5688, 186012, 186013; Grelin Whitebeard: 786, 119331, 119410). The classic-looking one was kept; verify.
- Magmatus and Plunder (Hall of Thanes): names only (from earlier doc 02); the wiki page for Magmatus returned 404 and the Hall of Thanes page has no boss list. No IDs, rarity guessed.
- Azar Stronghammer, Senir Whitebeard: no ID on the pages fetched. Houndmaster Loksey omitted (the page fetched was a different NPC).
- Rares Lost Soul and Tormented Spirit (Tirisfal) skipped: generic names, no ID checked. A New Plague and The Haunted Mills quest pages returned 404, so no ID.
- Rarity values are proposals; no Legendary candidate added since Magni (Dun Morogh) and Sylvanas (Tirisfal) are already shipped.
- Wowhead and browser tools were not used; IDs come only from the external links listed on wiki pages.
